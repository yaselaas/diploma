# Project Proposal
## Project title
Development of an Explainable AI-Based Decision Support System for Nature-Based Solutions and Urban Heat Island Mitigation in Astana
## Team and supervisor
[TO COMPLETE]
## Problem statement
Urban surface temperatures vary across built-up and vegetated areas. Local analysis can support evidence-informed planning.
## Target users
Urban planners, environmental specialists, researchers.
## Aim
Design and evaluate a geospatial decision support prototype for surface-heat analysis, explainable predictions and nature-based solution options in Astana.
## Objectives
1. Identify and prepare suitable geospatial data.
2. Establish baseline and ML models for a precisely defined target variable.
3. Explain predictions with XAI.
4. Design map, analysis and recommendation workflows.
5. Evaluate predictive quality, explanation fidelity and usability.
## Expected outcomes
Documented datasets, validated model comparison, explanations, prototype and evaluation report (planned).
## Scope and exclusions
Focus on historical land surface temperature and decision support; not real-time air-temperature forecasting or verified numerical cooling impacts of proposed interventions.
