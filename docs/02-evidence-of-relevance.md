# Evidence of Relevance
## Literature-based motivation
Use verified, properly cited studies from the literature review to support the general relevance of urban heat, urban greenery and explainable models. **Literature alone does not establish local user requirements.**
## Planned stakeholder questionnaire
1. What is your role?
2. How do you currently identify high-heat areas?
3. What information is missing from your workflow?
4. What datasets and maps do you use?
5. How useful would a map of land surface temperature be (1–5)?
6. Would explanations of AI predictions be useful, and why?
7. Which nature-based interventions are realistic in Astana?
8. Which practical constraints should recommendations show?
9. Which application functions are essential?
10. Would you be willing to review a prototype?
